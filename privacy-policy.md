## 1. Overview

**MyWOD Timer** ("the App") is a proprietary workout and interval timer application developed by Murat Ege for iOS and Android devices. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Free and PRO versions of the App.

---

## 2. Local Workout Data & On-Device Storage

MyWOD Timer does **not** require you to create an account or sign in to use its workout timers.

All workout-related content you create is stored **locally on your device** (using on-device storage technologies such as `Hive` and `SharedPreferences`), including:
- Custom workout timers, interval presets, and dashboard folder layouts
- Workout logs, split times, round/rep counts, and personal workout notes
- App preferences (audio themes, voice coach settings, language, and display settings)
- Recorded workout videos and HUD timer overlay exports

We do not upload your workout logs, personal notes, or recorded workout videos to our servers. You can delete your logs, cached files, and recorded videos at any time via **Settings → Storage & Media** or by uninstalling the App.

---

## 3. Device Permissions

MyWOD Timer requests the following permissions solely to deliver core application features:

1. **Camera (`NSCameraUsageDescription` / `android.permission.CAMERA`):** Used exclusively in the **Workout Video Camera (Recordings)** feature to record your workout sessions with a real-time on-screen digital timer HUD overlay. Video processing happens entirely on your device.
2. **Microphone (`NSMicrophoneUsageDescription` / `android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO`):** Used only during user-initiated workout video recording to capture audio alongside your video (can be muted anytime in the camera interface).
3. **Photo Library / Media Storage (`NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription` / `NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription`):** Used only when you choose to save, export, or share recorded workout videos to your device gallery or via the system share sheet.
4. **Background Audio & Wake Lock (`UIBackgroundModes: audio` / `WAKE_LOCK`):** Keeps the display awake during workouts, plays countdown beeps and spoken Voice Coach (`Text-to-Speech`) cues while the screen is locked or in the background, and smoothly ducks background music volume during alerts.
5. **Notifications & Vibration (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS` / `VIBRATE`):** Delivers local interval alerts and haptic feedback during active workouts.

## 4. Advertisements (Free Version) & Advertising Identifiers

The Free version of MyWOD Timer is supported by in-app advertisements (such as Google AdMob or authorized ad networks).

- **Data Used for Advertising:** Third-party advertising SDKs may collect or use device identifiers (such as Apple's Identifier for Advertisers `IDFA` on iOS or Google Advertising ID `AAID` on Android), coarse IP-based location, device model/OS version, and ad interaction data (impressions and clicks) to serve and measure ads.
- **Consent & Tracking Controls:**
  - **iOS (App Tracking Transparency - ATT):** On iOS 14.5+, the App requests your permission via Apple's system prompt before accessing the `IDFA` for personalized advertising. If you decline, only contextual (non-personalized) ads are shown.
  - **GDPR / KVKK / Regional Consent:** Users in the EEA, UK, Turkey, and other applicable regions are presented with consent options in accordance with local data protection regulations.
- **Removing Ads (MyWOD Timer PRO):** Upgrading to **MyWOD Timer PRO** disables in-app advertisements.

For more information on how Google uses data from partner apps, please visit [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## 5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions (MyWOD Timer PRO)

MyWOD Timer offers optional paid upgrades (**MyWOD Timer PRO**) to unlock restricted features, remove video watermarks, and remove in-app advertisements.

- All financial transactions are processed directly and securely by **Apple (App Store / StoreKit)** on iOS or **Google (Google Play Billing)** on Android.
- MyWOD Timer never collects, accesses, or stores your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing address. Only anonymized purchase receipts/tokens are verified to unlock PRO entitlements on your device.

## 6. Crash Reporting & Diagnostics

To ensure stability across devices, platform stores (Apple App Store / Google Play) or diagnostic tools may provide anonymized crash logs and performance metrics (device type, OS version, stack trace). These diagnostics do not contain personally identifiable information or workout videos.

## 7. Children's Privacy

MyWOD Timer is a general-audience fitness utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

## 8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / KVKK / CCPA)

Since your workout logs, custom routines, and recorded videos are stored locally on your device, you have full, immediate control to view, export (CSV/video), or permanently delete them inside the App. You may also reset or disable advertising identifiers at any time in your device's system settings (**iOS:** *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*; **Android:** *Settings → Google → Ads*).
