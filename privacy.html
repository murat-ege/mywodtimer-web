Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

We respect your privacy. This application does not collect, store, or share any personal data. All timer data and workout configurations are saved locally on your device.

Camera and Video Access

If you use the video recording features, the application processes the video locally to add watermarks. These videos remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
